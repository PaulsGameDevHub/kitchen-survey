# Kitchen Survey

An on-site checklist for kitchen surveys, in two parts:

1. **Room survey**: the room as it is now, basic measurements, water and waste, gas and electrics, the delivery route, parking and storage.
2. **New kitchen**: style, worktops, appliances (make, model and power needed), electrics and lighting, floor and finishing.

Questions open up as you answer. Anything that needs a second look goes on the follow-up list, and the finished survey can be saved as a PDF or copied as text.

Answers are saved only on the device filling them in. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Live at https://paulsgamedevhub.github.io/kitchen-survey/

## Changing the questions

Everything is in `index.html`, in the block between `SURVEY START` and `SURVEY END`. The comment above that block explains each setting.
